Install the extension

On a computer, in Chrome. About two minutes. You do this once.

  1. Download it

    Download the extension

  2. Unzip it

    Open your Downloads folder and double-click send-to-grok-bot.zip. You get a folder called send-to-grok-bot. Leave it there.

    On Windows: right-click the zip, then Extract All.

  3. Open Chrome's extensions page

    Copy this, paste it into the address bar at the top of Chrome, and press Enter.

    chrome://extensions

  4. Turn on Developer mode, then click Load unpacked

    1 Switch on Developer mode at the top right. 2 Click Load unpacked and choose the send-to-grok-bot folder from your Downloads.

  5. Come back here and refresh

    This page checks for the extension and moves you to the next step.
