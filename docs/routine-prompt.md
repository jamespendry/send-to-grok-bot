# Routine prompt

The prompt lives in [prompts/general.md](../prompts/general.md). Paste the text under the line in that file into a Grok Bot webhook routine or a Claude task. Do not paste the title.

Other presets are in [prompts/](../prompts/). The extension sends the preset you pick with the post, so the routine can keep the General prompt and still follow Research, Job hunt, Buy or skip, Learn later, or Content ideas.

The Claude clicks, including where Allowed domains actually is, are on [setup/claude.html](setup/claude.html).
