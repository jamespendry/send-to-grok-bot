Grok Bot

  1. Install Send to Grok Bot

    When Shortcuts opens, tap Add Shortcut, then come back here.

    If a file downloads instead, open this page in Safari and tap Install again. Installed an older version? Delete it in the Shortcuts app first, so you don't end up with "Send to Grok Bot (1)".

  2. Open Grok Bot

    Paste the message into any Grok Bot chat and send it. Grok Bot makes a routine named Send to Grok Bot.

    Already have that routine, for example from the Chrome extension? Skip this step.

  3. Connect your routine

    In Grok Bot, tap Grok Bot at the top of the chat, then Routines, then Send to Grok Bot. Scroll to Webhook.

    1. Tap the URL row to copy it. Come back, tap the box below, then tap Paste.
    2. Tap the Header row to copy it. Come back, tap the box, then tap Paste.

  4. Shortcuts opens for a moment and asks once to save a file. Tap Allow. You'll see Connected to Grok Bot.

    Finish turns on once the address and key are pasted.

  5. Sign in to the app

    Have the Claude app installed and signed in. That's all.

Send a post

In the X app, tap the share arrow under any post. Then:

A Sent to Grok Bot notice appears and you stay in X. Grok Bot's reply arrives in your Grok Bot chat.

Claude opens with the post and its thread filled in. Press Send.

Put it at the top of the list

  1. Open the share list the same way: share arrow, then Share via…
  2. Scroll to the very bottom and tap Edit Actions.
  3. Find Send to Grok Bot and tap the green plus next to it. Tap Done.

Once Grok Bot is connected, the Shortcut always sends to Grok Bot. Without it, the Shortcut asks which AI each time.

Codex works from the Chrome extension. On iPhone it only opens the website, so it is not offered.

The Shortcut sends the post link to sendtogrokbot.com to look up the post's text. For Grok Bot, the relay passes the post to your webhook; your webhook key is stored encrypted on the relay, which keeps a short list of recent sends (the link and author, not the text). Privacy
