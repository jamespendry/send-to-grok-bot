Setup

Connect Grok Bot

Three steps on a computer. Grok Bot creates the routine for you. You copy two things into the connect page.

  1. Send Grok Bot this line

    Set up Send to Grok Bot for me: https://github.com/jamespendry/send-to-grok-bot

    Paste it into any Grok Bot chat. It creates a routine named Send to Grok Bot and replies when it's done. If it asks you to confirm, confirm.

  2. Open the routine

    Click the bot's name at the top of the chat. Under Routines, click 1 Send to Grok Bot.

  3. Copy two values into the connect page

    Open the connect page
    1
    POST toStarts with https://api2.cursor.sh/automations/webhook/. Copy it and paste it into the connect page. It says "Webhook URL saved."
    2
    headerStarts with Authorization: Bearer crsr_. Copy it and paste it into the same box. It says "Now open x.com and click the arrow." The key line (just crsr_…) works too.

    If Chrome asks to allow a site, click Allow.

You're done. On x.com, click the arrow on any post. The icon turns green when the run starts, and the reply shows up in your Grok Bot chat.

Change the reply style later

Leave the routine as it is. In the extension settings, under Replies, pick Research, Job hunt, Buy or skip, Learn later, or Content ideas. The routine follows it on the next post.
