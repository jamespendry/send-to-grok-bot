Set up in 2 minutes

Send to Grok Bot adds one button on an X post. You click it and pick where the post goes. It is free, with nothing to buy and no account to create.

  1. Add the extension to Chrome

    A Send button appears next to Share on every post on x.com. Until it is in the Chrome Web Store, the button below opens the install steps: turn on Developer mode and load the folder named extension.

    Add to Chrome

  2. Pick your AI

    Choose one to start. Each takes you to its own short page.

    Grok Bot EasiestPaste one line into Grok Bot. It does the rest. ClaudeThree short steps on claude.ai. Runs in the background. ChatGPT No setupCheck one box. Posts open in ChatGPT, ready to send. Codex No setupCheck one box. Posts open as a Codex task.

How replies are written

Every send starts with a one-line takeaway. The default, General, works out what the post is first:

Tool, repo, or appWhat it does, whether it is worth installing, and how to try it.
Thread or long articleA three-line summary, then the key takeaway.
NewsWhat happened, why it matters, and what to watch.
VideoA description from the captions, thumbnail, and text. It does not claim to have watched.
Tip, prompt, or how-toOne step you can save.
Job, company, or founderWho they are, the stage, and who to follow.
Product or dealWorth buying or not.
Meme, opinion, or anything elseOne line of context, then it stops.

In the extension settings, under Replies, you can switch the style or edit the text. The one you pick is sent with the post.
