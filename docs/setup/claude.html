Setup

Claude

Two steps, about two minutes. After that, Claude replies to every post you send, on its own.

You need: a paid Claude plan (Pro or Max), and the extension added to Chrome with Send to Claude checked in its settings.

  1. Create the task on claude.ai

    Open Claude's Scheduled page. It opens in a new tab so you can keep this page open.

    Open Claude's Scheduled page

    At the top right, click 1 New task, then 2 Set up manually.

    Fill in the form, top to bottom:

    1
    NameType Send to Claude
    2
    InstructionsCopy this, then paste it into the big box.
    3
    TriggerClick Schedule and choose API.
    4
    CreateClick it. Leave everything else as it is.
    Open the extension settings and click Copy task prompt.

  2. Copy two things into the extension

    Right after you click Create, Claude shows a 1 Fire URL and a 2 Token.

    1. Open the connect page in a new tab.
    2. Copy the Fire URL and paste it into the box.
    3. Copy the Token and paste it into the same box, on a new line.
    4. Click Connect. If Chrome asks to allow api.anthropic.com, click Allow.
    Copy the token now. Claude shows it only once. Keep it private: don't paste it into a chat or an email.
You're done. Open x.com, refresh, click the Send button on any post, and choose Send to Claude. The icon turns green when Claude starts.

Where Claude's replies show up

On Claude's Scheduled page, open Send to Claude. Each post you send is a line under History. Click one to read Claude's reply.

Lost the token, or Claude says it's invalid?
  1. Open Send to Claude on the Scheduled page.
  2. Click the small arrow next to its name at the top, then Edit.
  3. Under Trigger, click Regenerate and copy the new token. The old one stops working.
  4. Paste the new token into the connect page and click Connect.
Rather skip all of this?

Check Send to Claude in the extension and leave the Fire URL box empty. Clicking a post opens Claude in a new tab with the post filled in, and you press Send. Works on any plan, including free.
