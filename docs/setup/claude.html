Setup

Connect Claude

Two ways to connect Claude. Pick one. After that, Claude replies to every post you send, on its own.

Do it yourselfAbout 4 minutes on claude.ai. Works with any browser setup. Have Claude do itNeeds Claude in Chrome. You add the extension and click Allow.
Either way you need: a paid Claude plan (Pro or Max).

Do it yourself

  1. Start a new task

    Open Claude's Scheduled page

    At the top right, click 1 New task, then 2 Set up manually.

  2. Fill in the form

    1
    NameType Send to Claude
    2
    InstructionsCopy this, then paste it into the big box.
    3
    TriggerClick Schedule and choose API.
    4
    CreateClick it. Leave everything else as it is.
    Open the extension settings and click Copy task prompt.

  3. Paste your Claude key on the connect page

    Open the connect page

    Back on Claude, your key is showing, in a box called API token. Click 1 the copy button and paste it into the connect page's box. It saves as soon as you paste. Then click 2 Done.

  4. Paste the task link too

    Claude opens your new task. Under Triggers, click 1 the copy button next to API. Paste it into the same box on the connect page. If Chrome asks to allow api.anthropic.com, click Allow.

Keep the token private. Paste it only on the connect page, never in a chat or an email.
You're done. Open x.com, refresh, click the Send arrow on any post, and choose Send to Claude. The icon turns green when Claude starts.
Have Claude do it instead

Claude creates the task on claude.ai and connects the extension. It never sees your token. You need Claude in Chrome connected to this browser.

  1. Send Claude this line

    Set up Send to Grok Bot for me, with Claude: https://github.com/jamespendry/send-to-grok-bot

  2. Click Allow when Chrome asks

    Claude opens the connect page and clicks Finish. Chrome then asks to let the extension reach api.anthropic.com. Click Allow.

Where Claude's replies show up

On Claude's Scheduled page, open Send to Claude. Each post you send is a line under History. Click one to read Claude's reply.

Lost the token, or Claude says it's invalid?
  1. On the Scheduled page, open Send to Claude.
  2. Click the small arrow next to its name at the top, then Edit.
  1. Under Trigger, click Regenerate and copy the new token. The old one stops working.
  2. Paste the new token on the connect page. It saves when you paste.
Rather skip all of this?

Check Send to Claude in the extension and leave the Fire URL box empty. Clicking a post opens Claude in a new tab with the post filled in, and you press Send. Works on any plan, including free.
