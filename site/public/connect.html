Connect

Paste the link you were given, or the address and the secret from Grok Bot or Claude. Then click Connect.

If Chrome asks to allow a site, choose Allow. Then open x.com, refresh, and click the Send button on a post.

This page stays in your browser. It does not send the address or the secret anywhere except into the Send to Grok Bot extension on this computer, and only after you click Connect.

ChatGPT and Codex need none of this. Check the box in the extension and click Save. A post opens with the text filled in, and you press Send.
