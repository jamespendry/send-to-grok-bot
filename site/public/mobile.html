Send to Grok Bot
Setup iPhone Add to Chrome

On iPhone

Send posts from the X app with the share sheet. It uses an iOS Shortcut, so there is no app to install.

Before you start: set up Grok Bot or Claude on your computer first, and keep your relay code handy. It is one line that starts with stgbr1. You get it once from relay.sendtogrokbot.com.

  1. Add the Shortcut

    Open this on your iPhone and tap Add Shortcut.

    Get the Shortcut Link coming soon

  2. Paste your relay code

    The Shortcut asks for it once while it installs. Paste the whole code and tap Done. Keep it private. Anyone with it can send posts to your AI.

  3. Share a post

    In the X app, tap the share icon on a post, then Share via, and pick Send to Grok Bot. The first time, iOS asks to allow it. Tap Always Allow.

The Shortcut sends to Grok Bot or Claude, whichever you saved with your relay code. ChatGPT and Codex work in Chrome on your computer.
