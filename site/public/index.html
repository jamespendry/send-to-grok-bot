How it works

Set up in 2 minutes

Add to Chrome

Install the extension. A Send button appears next to Share on every post.

Pick your AI

Grok Bot, Claude, ChatGPT, or Codex. ChatGPT and Codex need no setup at all.

Click Send

The icon turns green. Your AI replies with what the post means for you.

Start setup

A reply that fits the post

Every reply starts with a one-line takeaway. Then it answers the question you would have asked.

A tool or appWhat it does, whether it is worth installing, and how to try it.
A thread or articleA three-line summary and the key takeaway.
A news storyWhat happened, why it matters, and what to watch.
A product or dealWorth buying or not, and why.
A tip or how-toOne step you can save and use.

Six reply styles

Switch any time in the extension settings. General is the default.

Private by design

Only the post you click is sent, and only to the AI you pick. Keys stay in your browser. No analytics, no tracking, nothing sold.
