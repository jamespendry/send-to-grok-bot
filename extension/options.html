More options

Clicking Send on a post reads that post and, on its own page, the author's thread and the post it replies to. That includes the text, link, author, quoted post, links, and media addresses the page already shows, plus the reply preset you pick below. It is sent only to the destination you pick. Keys and tokens stay on this device and are sent only to sign in to that destination. No analytics. Nothing is sold. Click Save to store these settings on this device.

Replies

General is the default. It classifies the post and starts with a one-line takeaway. Pick another preset, or edit the text and it becomes Custom. The choice is sent with the post. Paste General into a Grok Bot routine or a Claude task once, and this page can switch the reply later.
